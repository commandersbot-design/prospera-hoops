Prospera Hoops is the home for high school and AAU basketball across DC, Maryland, and Virginia — real stats, real film, and real exposure to the college coaches who decide a player's future. Free for every player. Built on one promise: only the truth of the game, never pay-to-play hype.

The problem

The DMV's talent is everywhere — and nowhere

One of the richest basketball regions in America, scattered across dozens of leagues with no central home. National recruiting sites charge for rankings and still miss hundreds of local kids. The next great player goes unseen because no one is looking in the right place.

Our answer

One home for the whole region

Players claim a free profile, show verified game stats and film, and get discovered. Coaches and programs scout the entire DMV from one place — with real data instead of paid hype. Every player on the map; every coach in the gym.
