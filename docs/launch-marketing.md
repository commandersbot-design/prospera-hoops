## 2. Should you post before launch? YES.

A launch into empty accounts converts terribly. Spend **Sun–Tue seeding + building a list** so
Wednesday lands on an audience instead of silence. Pre-launch posts don't say "go use it" (site
isn't live) — they say **"this is coming, get in first."**

Goal of pre-launch: **(a)** ~10–15 posts of content so the grid isn't empty, **(b)** a waitlist of
players who want their profile, **(c)** the "Founding 100" promo seeded (below).

## 3. The promotion — "The Founding 100"

Scarcity + status + a real prize. Costs you almost nothing and creates urgency at the games.

> **The first 100 DMV players to claim their Prospera profile get:**
> - A permanent **"Founding Player"** badge on their profile (exclusive, never offered again)
> - A free custom **highlight stat-card** (the shareable trading card)
> - Entry to win **[PRIZE]**

**Pick your prize (cheapest → splashiest):**
1. **Free** — a **featured spotlight** post (you post their card to all channels) + the badge. Status only. Genuinely enough.
2. **~$50–100** — a gift card (Hoop Culture / Dick's / Eastbay) or a gear drop. Add this for buzz.
3. **Product-tied** — a free **"Prospera Verified"** measurement (height/wingspan/vert) when you run a combine. Best long-term, ties prize to the moat.

**Multiplier (referral):** "Claim **+** tag 3 teammates who also claim = 2× your entry." This is what
makes it spread on a roster.

**The honesty line (keep your brand):** the badge is real and permanent; don't promise rankings or
stats you can't back. "Founding Player" = *was here first*, not *is ranked #1*.

## 4. The in-person game playbook (your superpower, Wed+)

You're physically at the games where these kids and parents are. Convert on the spot.

**Before you go (Tuesday):**
- Print a **QR code** that opens `prosperahoops.com` (I can generate the PNG — see end). Put it on:
  a phone lock-screen, a few printed cards/flyers, and a shirt/sticker if you can.
- Have the **claim flow ready** and your phone logged in.

**At the game (the loop that goes viral):**
1. **Film a kid's bucket** on your phone (vertical, 10–20s).
2. Walk up: *"That was clean — want your stats tracked? Scan this, you'll have a profile in 2 min, free."* → QR.
3. They (or a parent) claim → they're a **Founding Player**.
4. That night, **load their game's box score** → their **stat card is ready** → DM it / post it tagging them.
5. They **repost it** → their teammates see it → those teammates claim. **That's the engine.**

**Capture everything:** jot rosters + scores (or grab the scorebook photo). Every game you cover →
a team page + recaps + cards = content for days and a reason for that whole team to follow.

**Content from being there:** "Scouted live at [event] 🏀📊", sideline clips, "today's top performer,"
behind-the-scenes. Authenticity > polish — *being the person at the games* IS the brand.

## 7. Do's & don'ts
- **DO** tag the player + their HS + their AAU on every card (they all repost → reach).
- **DO** reply to every comment/DM fast in week 1 (the algorithm + the trust both reward it).
- **DO** keep the honest voice — "real stats, no fake rankings." It's your differentiator vs hype accounts.
- **DON'T** post empty/low-effort to hit a number — one great card > five weak posts.
- **DON'T** post minors' contact info or social handles (privacy — your own rule).
