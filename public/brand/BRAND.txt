PROSPERA HOOPS - Brand assets
================================

COLORS
  Signal Orange  #FF6A1A   primary accent / the basketball
  Graphite       #0B0E13   backgrounds, seams
  Ink            #11151C   text on light
  Bar ramp       #9A3E12 > #C24A14 > #E0531B > #FF6A1A
  White          #FFFFFF

FONT
  Saira Condensed ExtraBold (800) - free on Google Fonts.
  SVGs embed the font, so they render correctly anywhere.

WHAT GOES WHERE
  symbol-512            app icon / Vercel / general mark
  symbol-32 / -16       favicon
  avatar-circle-512     IG / TikTok / YouTube profile pic
  mark-square-512       square app tiles
  wordmark-dark-1600w   social posts on dark
  wordmark-light-1600w  on light backgrounds
  lockup-dark-1600w     site header (dark)
  lockup-light-1600w    site header (light)
  symbol-bare           secondary mark - use only alongside the full name
  svg/*                 scalable source for every asset

RULES
  The symbol carries the basketball, so the header lockup uses plain
  PROSPERA / HOOPS. The standalone wordmark uses the ball as the O.
  Keep clear space around the logo equal to the basketball's height.
